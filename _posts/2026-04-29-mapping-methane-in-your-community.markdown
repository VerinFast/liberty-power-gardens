## The Hidden Emissions in Our Neighborhoods

Every day, our communities release methane into the air—from landfills, wastewater treatment, and food waste decomposition. But here's what's changed: we can now *see* it. Satellite technology and AI-powered detection systems are finally revealing where these invisible emissions are strongest, and what that means for neighborhoods like yours.

## Breaking the Invisibility Problem

For decades, methane emissions were a blind spot. We knew they were happening, but pinpointing *where* and *how much* was nearly impossible. Now, companies and researchers are using satellites, drones, and LiDAR technology to create detailed maps of methane hotspots—including the ones hiding in plain sight in urban areas.

The data is eye-opening. Cities and towns across the globe are discovering that their actual methane footprint is far larger than previously estimated. In some cases, emissions are 15 to 40 percent higher than what officials thought.

## What This Means for Your Community

This detection revolution creates an opportunity. When we know *exactly* where emissions are coming from, we can do something about it. Community-scale biodigestion systems can capture methane from food waste and organic materials—turning what would have become atmosphere-bound gas into usable energy.

## From Data to Action

The science is clear: methane is 20+ times more potent than CO₂ over a 20-year period. But it's also highly *captureable*. When your community works together to capture and repurpose these emissions—through food waste diversion, anaerobic digestion, or biogas energy systems—you're not just reducing emissions. You're building local energy resilience and keeping resources in your community instead of losing them to the atmosphere.

## Taking the Next Step

The question isn't whether your community has a methane problem. The question is: are you ready to solve it? The technology exists. The data exists. Now it's about choosing to act.
